# References

These references frame the institutional control, evidence automation, and tokenized-asset context for `control-probe`. They are not endorsements and do not make this template an audit, legal, or regulatory opinion.

## Control Evidence And Automation

- [NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5, Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final): control catalog and risk-management context for system controls.
- [NIST OSCAL, Open Security Controls Assessment Language](https://pages.nist.gov/OSCAL/): machine-readable control, assessment, and evidence formats.

## Tokenized Asset And Cryptoasset Risk Context

- [BIS Annual Economic Report 2025, Chapter III: The next-generation monetary and financial system](https://www.bis.org/publ/arpdf/ar2025e3.htm): tokenisation and financial-market infrastructure context.
- [CPMI, Tokenisation in the context of money and other assets](https://www.bis.org/cpmi/publ/d225.htm): tokenisation concepts, programmable platforms, governance, and risk-management considerations.
- [Basel Committee, Prudential treatment of cryptoasset exposures](https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d545.htm): banking prudential context for cryptoasset and tokenised traditional asset exposures.
